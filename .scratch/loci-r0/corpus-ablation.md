# R0 fixed-anchor corpus ablations — 2026-10-22

**Result.** With the frozen gold anchor supplied in both arms, the existing graph-off seam shrinks the normal retrieval packets substantially but removes almost all other required source spans and every relationship. Suppressing only representative members preserves most context, but loses six required spans in each corpus and five credited relationship links overall for modest packet savings. Neither candidate is an across-the-board improvement on this selected, single-packet task context. These are deterministic mechanism results, not agent-completion or provider-cost results.

Graph-off's packet bytes fall 69.7% for TypeScript and 78.4% for multilingual, with source losses in 17/17 and 18/19 cases respectively. Its zero relationship score is designed into this control; it says what graph enrichment supplied to one packet, not whether graph construction/index loading could be removed. In the direct path, graph state was already loaded; the [separate full-service validation study](validation-ablation.md) shows repeated graph reconstruction dominates larger-repository calls and a guarded cache saved only 1% on retrieval. Do not add that study's time to this table or infer full host latency from these numbers.

Member suppression reduces packet bytes 4.4% and 13.0% for the two corpora. It loses source in four TypeScript cases (`local_alias_chain`, `imported_alias_chain`, `ambiguous_star_exports`, `anvil_temporal_arguments`) and five multilingual cases (`python_unproven_contracts`, `go_unproven_package_uses`, `rust_authored_trait_contract`, `rust_unproven_contracts`, `python_loci_bundle_contract`). Credited links fall in `local_alias_chain`, `imported_alias_chain`, `anvil_temporal_arguments` (one each) and `rust_authored_trait_contract` (two). No case gains a required gold source span or credited link. Two multilingual packets grow slightly (+237 and +105 bytes); changes to omission/state serialization can offset removed members. Detailed per-case requests, source IDs, scores, omissions, bytes, graph work counters, all failed-attempt fields and three timings are in the [control results](corpus-control-direct/results.json) and [ownership results](corpus-ownership-direct/results.json); complete raw packets are adjacent `packets.jsonl` files.

Five baseline semantic call claims (three Anvil/TypeScript, two maintained Loci/Python) were present in valid normal packet shapes but uncredited by the historical proof scorer for incomplete persisted-support coverage. The same five remain in the member-off packets. They are recorded as scorer `delivery_integrity_violations` in the case results, not promoted to false authored edges or zero-cost successes. The independent source/evidence-line check above passed; resolving the stricter support discrepancy is outside this bounded architectural comparison. Structural `imports`/`imports_type` relations remain in every raw packet and passed the independent source/evidence check; they were filtered **only in memory** before the semantic gold scorer because that scorer excludes structural edges by contract. The five strict proof violations and the eight/eleven baseline missing gold spans mean even the full-enrichment condition is incomplete on this selected single-request measure.

The visible timing benefit of member suppression is mixed for TypeScript: its median case improves slightly, while its sum of case medians is 1,048 vs 1,018 ms because of the maintained Anvil cases. Multilingual median falls from 10.19 to 7.44 ms. Fixed condition order, three warm repetitions and concurrent host work limit timing inference. TypeScript's one maintained Anvil snapshot dominates its summed time; no population or stable speed claim follows. Index/setup costs are outside the table: control TypeScript indexing totaled 4,769 ms with 1,417 ms preload (Anvil ~4,596/1,399 ms), and multilingual totaled 321/36 ms. These are one fresh pass per worktree, not paired indexing ablations.

## Disposition and retained artifacts

- **Graph-off:** useful as an isolation control. As currently implemented, retaining only 1,024-byte anchor previews does not cover this corpus's fixed gold context. It still constructs/loads graph state. A source-first architecture that reallocates the packet to complete target source or supports targeted follow-up would be a different experiment; this result does not refute one.
- **Representative-member removal:** blanket suppression is questionable. It saves some output and local traversal work, but loses specific source and proof that was useful under frozen gold. More selective staging would need a separately defined mechanism and gold; this test does not authorize a product edit.
- **Integrity boundary:** exact source bytes, hashes, bounds, proof evidence and explicit omissions passed the packet checks and remain justified. The experiment cannot justify deleting parsers, language resolvers, diagnostic relationship tools, or native file access. Native tools can read Go/Cargo control files even where this one packet omits them; graph-delivered control source is not proof the graph is required for the full task.
