# R0.4 proof-support audit (read-only)

The five R0 normal-retrieval discrepancies have one mechanism. Each delivered `calls` edge has exactly one resolved persisted call record, and its call site, owner interval, source hash, and other support match the frozen source. Normal retrieval deliberately omits the large `caller_definition` from each relationship's proof source IDs after adding an exact call-site span (`src/loci/_retrieval_source.py:106-135` at implementation revision `8b21db82cb5d249336b5d3e9936e0df518b185ea`). The normal packet still labels the relationship `proof: "complete"` (`src/loci/_retrieval_output.py:199-227`). The caller item is a partial preview. The runner subsequently reads that item's `source_ref` to completion (`benchmarks/architecture.py:149-164`), so the full caller definition is present in the retained *case session*, with correct bytes and hash. The frozen strict semantic scorers assess an exploration-style relationship against only its relation source IDs and reject it.

This is a delivery/scorer-adapter scope mismatch. It does **not** establish that any of the five call edges is false. Under the recorded per-relationship proof contract, each claim remains incomplete and its case remains invalid. The original full comparison is `invalid`, both paired suites are `unavailable`, and this audit does not rescore or relabel either (`.scratch/r0-evaluation/results/full/result.json`). One `_validate_bundle` full caller span also appears as source 11 in the initial packet, but belongs to another relationship's source list; that makes it globally available without completing this edge's own relation source IDs.

The [machine-readable audit](proof-audit.json) gives the full edge identities, relation source IDs, each persisted support kind/interval/hash, preview and continuation ranges, and archive, packet, index, capability and retained evaluator SHA-256 digests. It records `relation_covered: false` **only** on the `caller_definition` interval for each edge; `all_packets_covered: true` on every support interval. Every delivered span was checked byte-for-byte against its frozen archive member and whole-file hash. Each raw call site's byte range falls inside the uniquely matched caller symbol and the persisted raw owner range exactly equals that indexed symbol's extent. The replay used `inputs/0/anvil.tar.gz` and `inputs/1/fixtures.tar.gz`, the original `control-1-suite-{0,1}/packets`, their compressed indexes, and the retained evaluator sources under `results/full/evaluator/benchmarks/`. The TypeScript and multilingual scorer bytes match those in the current checkout; the current architecture adapter has R0.4 additions after the retained original. The capability SHA-256 digests match `.scratch/r03-exact-source/unresolved-semantic-proofs.json` (`00a59a...` and `b1624a...`).

The adapter at `benchmarks/architecture_cases.py:161-186` validates normal packet relationship source IDs, then relabels the packet with `intent: "type_dependencies"`. The TypeScript scorer checks indexed support solely against those IDs (`benchmarks/typescript_context_explore_relationships.py:131-158`). The multilingual scorer receives the globally validated session spans, but chooses relation sources for such exploration-labeled claims (`benchmarks/multilingual_context_relationships.py:547-556`). That explains why the continuation reads do not alter the frozen score. A full caller read elsewhere in the session does not retroactively add it to the packet's relationship `source_ids`.

For R0.4, preserve the existing strict per-claim score and the original invalid comparison. If a separate **aggregate-session proof** measure is needed, construct it only from the same case's byte/hash-validated normal packet and completed continuation reads, keeping edge identity, persisted resolved record, call-site/owner binding, and every remaining support/control requirement. Invoke the unchanged strict scorer on that explicitly scoped evidence. Require a negative check that removes the full caller extent from **all** sources in that case session, including any initial packet copies: aggregate-session credit must disappear while the historical per-relationship failure remains. A source span or a graph-free exact read alone earns source-acquisition credit only; it cannot establish a semantic call without the corresponding independently checked persisted semantic record and proof. Do not report task-answer correctness from these packets.
