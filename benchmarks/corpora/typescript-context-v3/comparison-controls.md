# V3 comparison controls

Declared before new measured runs. The binding JSON retains v2 numerical gates,
source tasks, source gold, model route and limits. Its read metric is newly named:
**observed tool calls**, not v1/v2 declared source-supported causal recovery.
Do not pair or rescore historical v1/v2 measurements under this contract.

A is existing search/get and explicit graph retrieval at production 9acd3e3,
extractor 24. B adds bounded selection/hydration over existing relationships.
C adds proven TypeScript relationships to the same policy as B. B and C do not
exist yet; pin their commits and intended diffs before their measured requests.
All arms expose the same 13 typed logical retrieval operations, same source
snapshot boundary, ranking, K=5, filters and budgets. A gets no automatic expansion.

Run GPT-5.6 Luna high through Codex 0.154.0 and the existing ChatGPT login. Fresh
isolated session, source snapshot and prebuilt index/store per attempt; measure
cold indexing separately. Audit effective model-visible input/tool schemas before
each run. No gold, ambient Brain, skills, earlier answers, delegation, external
source access or repository execution. Preserve raw source and configuration hashes,
provider usage and exact output. The unversioned slug does not pin backend weights.

Use 17 cases x3 repetitions per arm, serial, corpus order. A-only has 51 attempts.
A/B has 102, case orders A/B, B/A, A/B. A/B/C has 153, orders A/B/C, B/C/A, C/A/B.
Future comparisons rerun every comparator arm in a fresh matched batch. Environment
changes require a new full matched environment block; do not mix revisions.

Count every observed top-level retrieval-workflow tool call, including discovery,
hydration, verification, empty/error/helper calls and duplicates. See
[exact observation accounting](observation-protocol.md). A recoverable bounded
input error may be followed by a valid correct answer, with its cost retained.
Hard-budget violations, process/time failures, missing measurements or unverified
source/output cannot pass. A wrong answer cannot become efficient by stopping early.

Candidate acceptance requires 42/42 fixture full passes, zero authored unsupported
proven relationships across all cases, preserved repaired endpoints, at least 8/9
maintained full passes and no task success regression. A full pass requires the
exact answer, complete recorded measurements and compliance with hard limits.

Read reduction uses tasks whose three repetitions in both compared arms fully
pass. Require at least two such maintained tasks. For each eligible task take the
median of its three observed-call counts, then sum those medians. Require a positive
comparator sum, at least 20% reduction, and at least one fewer median call on two of
the three tasks. These numerical gates are unchanged; the denominator is explicitly
all observed calls, including planned work, rather than an inferred avoidable subset.

Across all three maintained tasks, including failures, require no reduction in each
task's median exact required-source recall; no increase in summed per-task median
complete output bytes or gross input tokens; and p95 end-to-end latency no more
than 1.25 times comparator. Use nearest-rank p95 across all 9 including capped timeouts.
Report all raw outcomes and group/per-task medians. These three analysis tasks from
one maintained repository do not establish broad coding or code-edit gains.

Compare B against A, and C against both B and A. C's incremental read benefit must
meet the same rule against B; if B was rejected, C can pass against A while benefit
from new semantics remains unproven unless C-versus-B also passes. No threshold,
gold, model or scoring changes after answers. Any changed contract gets a new
version before measurement. No correctness retries. A confirmed provider/host
outage permits at most one replacement of the whole matched case/repetition block,
retaining the original. A second outage leaves the batch incomplete. Unknown
required evidence is inconclusive, never zero or a pass.
